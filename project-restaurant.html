Restaurant Sales Optimization

May 2026
GitHub Repository

1. Problem Statement

Analyzing 500+ order transactions spanning an 8-month period to determine peak revenue hours, profitable menu items, and optimize operational staffing decisions.

2. Approach & Tools Used

  • Data Normalization: Cleaned transactional Excel data, organized field headers, and separated date/time metrics.
  • Excel Analytics & Pivot Tables: Utilized nested formulas, dynamic Pivot Tables, and slicers to evaluate sales performance.
  • Dashboard Visuals: Built an interactive Excel dashboard highlighting total revenue ($20K+ tracked) and average order value (AOV).

3. Key Insights

  • Peak Revenue Hours: Highest order volume occurred between 7:00 PM and 10:00 PM.
  • Category Profitability: Core meal items generated 80% profit margins, driving overall monthly gross profit.
  • Day-of-Week Trends: Weekends experienced a 35% increase in total revenue compared to weekday averages.

4. Business Recommendations

  • Reallocate shift scheduling to increase kitchen staff during peak evening hours (7 PM - 10 PM).
  • Create combo promotional offers pairing high-margin items with lower-performing side dishes during weekdays.
